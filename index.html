FAQ

Common questions.

What size of brand do you work with?

Most of our clients spend between $20k and $500k per month on paid media and do $100k to $5M in monthly revenue. If you're earlier than that but have product-market fit and strong margins, apply anyway and we'll tell you honestly whether it makes sense.

How do you charge?

A flat monthly retainer scaled to your spend and scope, plus an optional performance component tied to contribution margin growth. No percentage-of-spend incentives that reward us for spending more.

Do you handle creative production?

Yes. Strategy, scripting, copy, editing, and static design are in-house. For UGC and studio shoots we work with a vetted network of creators and production partners.

How soon will we see results?

Weeks one and two are audit and rebuild. Most clients see CPA improvements within 30 days and meaningful scale by day 60 to 90. We forecast this for you before we start.

Is there a minimum contract?

An initial 90-day engagement so the rebuild has time to work, then month-to-month with 30 days' notice.
